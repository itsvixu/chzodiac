Illustrating the signs

Two ways to draw the same Monkey and Rooster. Both are plain vector shapes animated with CSS, so they stay sharp and light in in-app browsers. Pick one and the other ten signs follow in that style.

Direction A

Star-line

Gold line drawings that trace themselves on, like a constellation being joined up. Leans into the space theme; quiet and elegant.

  • Motion: draws on once at the reveal, then a slow idle (tail, ears, blink).
  • Best for: a calm, premium feel that keeps the hanzi as the hero.
  • Watch out: thin lines read less well at small sizes and on the share card.

Direction B · recommended

Paper-cut

Flat cinnabar and gold shapes, after the red paper cuttings hung in windows at New Year. Warm, festive and instantly readable.

  • Motion: pops in, then loops with character: the monkey waves its peach and swings its tail, the rooster struts and fans its feathers.
  • Best for: shareability. Bold shapes hold up on the card and at thumbnail size.
  • Watch out: more playful, so the hanzi becomes a supporting seal rather than the hero.

Concept sketches to agree a direction, not final artwork. Animation pauses for visitors who ask their device to reduce motion.
